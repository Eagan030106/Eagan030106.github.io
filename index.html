About关于我

Product thinking · Service design产品思维 · 服务设计

I define the problem before the product. Every project starts with three questions: what users actually struggle with, whether technology can solve it reliably, and whether the business can sustain it. From home retinal screening to the smart cockpit, I turn research into clear requirements, testable prototypes and solutions that can ship.我习惯先定义问题，再定义产品。每个项目都从三个问题出发：用户真正的痛点是什么，技术能否稳定地解决它，商业上能否持续。从居家视网膜筛查到智能座舱，我负责把研究洞察转化为清晰的需求、可验证的原型和可落地的方案。

01

Define the problem定义问题

Field research, interviews and data to find the need worth solving, then a clear problem statement and scope.通过田野调研、访谈和数据找到值得解决的需求，形成清晰的问题定义与范围。

02

Validate early尽早验证

Structured experiments with stated goals and hypotheses, so decisions rest on evidence rather than taste.设定目标与假设的结构化实验，让决策建立在证据上，而不是个人偏好。

03

Deliver across teams跨团队交付

Journey maps, system maps and specs that engineering, business and partners can act on together.用旅程图、系统图和设计规范，让工程、业务与合作方在同一份方案上推进。
