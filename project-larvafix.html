LARVAFIX:
Pipe Repair Without DiggingLARVAFIX：
不开挖的管道修复

A soft robot that repairs bent city pipes from the inside, modelled on how shipworms tunnel, together with the system and service around it, so streets don't have to be dug up.一个从内部修复城市弯管的软体机器人，模仿船蛆钻洞的方式；同时设计了围绕它的系统与服务，让修管道不再需要开挖马路。

Pipes wear out 20–45 years before the city above them. Neighbourhoods built in the 1990s–2000s are in their first wave of failures.管网比地面早 20–45 年到期，1990–2000 年代建成的社区正迎来第一轮老化。

Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Buildings and roads last 50 to 70 years, but the pipes beneath them are designed for 25 to 30. Many neighbourhoods built in the 1990s and 2000s now face their first wave of failures.地面建筑和道路寿命一般是 50 到 70 年，而地下管网的设计寿命往往只有 25 到 30 年。许多 1990 和 2000 年代建成的社区正迎来第一轮大规模老化。
  • Ethnography and case studies across the US, China, Japan and the UK showed that elbows cause 40 to 60 percent of leaks and failures, and that existing repair robots struggle with bends.对美国、中国、日本和英国的民族志与案例研究发现：弯头占漏损和故障的 40% 至 60%，而现有修复机器人很难处理弯管。

  1. 01 Steer转向to the damage到达破损处
  2. 02 Inflate充气the air column撑起气柱
  3. 03 Release释放cement-soaked fabric浸水泥的无纺布
  4. 04 Set & recover固化回收cement sets, column retracts水泥固化后回收气柱
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Steer to the damage, inflate the air column, release cement-soaked non-woven fabric, then let the cement set and recover the air column.控制转向到达破损处，充起气柱，释放浸有水泥的无纺布，待水泥固定后回收气柱。
