Digestion:
An External Organ for Information DetoxDigestion：
信息排毒的体外器官

A wearable "external information stomach" that reads the colours of what you watch on your phone and shows, through light and video, how well your mind is digesting it.一个可穿戴的「体外信息胃」：读取你在手机上看的内容的颜色，再用光和影像显示你的大脑消化得怎么样。

Role角色
Personal project个人项目

11/2025 – 01/2026
Context背景
Critical design, external organ device思辨设计 · 体外器官装置
Methods & tools方法与工具
RGB colour sensor, Arduino, LED strips, PLA tube and film, resin, LED screens, video

01/ 04Spark起点

Sixteen hours on a phone一天刷了 16 个小时手机

  • 16 h16 小时of videos in one day, where the project started一天刷视频的时长，作品由此开始

Diagnosis判断

Not too little information but information obesity: algorithms serve mental fast food.问题不是信息太少，而是「信息肥胖」：推荐算法提供的是精神快餐。

Read the notes展开文字说明
  • It started with a day spent watching videos for 16 hours. It felt like my brain had been fed a pile of broken, unpalatable food.起点是某一天我刷了 16 个小时视频，感觉大脑像被喂了一堆破碎又难以下咽的食物。
  • We no longer lack information; we suffer from "information obesity", and recommendation algorithms serve mental fast food.我们不再缺少信息，而是陷入「信息肥胖」，推荐算法提供的是精神快餐。

02/ 04Thinking设计思考

The mind digests like a stomach大脑像胃一样消化信息

How the mind copes大脑的反应

  • Information overload信息过载
  • Cognitive load认知负荷
  • Heuristic thinking启发式思维
  • Halo effect光环效应
  • Behaviour行为输出

How a stomach copes胃的反应

  • Reflux反酸
  • Bloating胀气
  • Emergency mode应激
  • Overeating暴食

Against self-domestication反自我驯化

  • Friction摩擦
  • Confusion困惑
  • Metabolism代谢
  • Forgetting遗忘
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Information overload, cognitive load, heuristic thinking, the halo effect and behaviour were mapped onto the stomach: reflux, bloating, emergency mode and overeating.把信息过载、认知负荷、启发式思维、光环效应和行为输出，一一对应到胃的反应：反酸、胀气、应激和暴食。
  • The design takes a stand against "self-domestication" by algorithms, using friction, confusion, metabolism and forgetting.作品以「反自我驯化」为立场，用摩擦、困惑、代谢与遗忘对抗算法的全景凝视。

03/ 04Translation信息转化

From screen time to stomach acid从屏幕时间到胃酸颜色

Screen time, read by an RGB sensorRGB 传感器读取的屏幕时间

  • Education学习
  • Entertainment娱乐
  • Social社交
pH colour scalepH 色卡
  1. 01 Ingest摄入colour sensor颜色传感器
  2. 02 Digest消化luminous gastric tube发光胃管
  3. 03 Absorb吸收external stomach体外信息胃
  4. 04 Excrete排泄video output影像输出
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Screen-time categories are read by an RGB sensor and mapped onto a pH colour scale: green for education, orange for entertainment, blue for social.RGB 传感器读取屏幕使用时间的分类颜色，再映射到 pH 色卡：绿色代表学习，橙色代表娱乐，蓝色代表社交。
  • Four stages make up the device: ingest (colour sensor), digest (luminous gastric tube), absorb (the external stomach) and excrete (video output).装置由四个阶段组成：摄入（颜色传感器）、消化（发光胃管）、吸收（体外信息胃）和排泄（影像输出）。

04/ 04Making制作与交互

Calm or restless平静或躁动

Calm平静

When条件Green dominates绿色占多数

Then表现The stomach stays calm胃保持平静

Restless躁动

When条件Orange and blue pile up橙色和蓝色堆积

Then表现Light turns red, video turns turbulent灯光变红，影像躁动

Built with材料与技术

  • PLA tube & filmPLA 管与薄膜
  • Resin树脂
  • LED stripsLED 灯带
  • Screens屏幕
  • Arduino
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Built from PLA tube and film, resin, LED strips and screens, programmed with Arduino after material, shape and hemming tests.经过材料、造型和封边测试后，用 PLA 管与薄膜、树脂、LED 灯带和屏幕制作，并用 Arduino 编程。
  • When green dominates the stomach stays calm. When orange and blue pile up, the light turns red and the video becomes turbulent, like a stomach in distress.绿色占多数时，胃保持平静；橙色和蓝色堆积时，灯光变红，影像变得躁动，就像一个不舒服的胃。
