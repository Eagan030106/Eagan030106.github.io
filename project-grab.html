Grab Price Shock Recovery:
Keeping Riders After a Price ShockGrab 价格冲击补救：
糟糕的计价之后，留住乘客

Churn often starts with one bad fare. The service steps in at three moments and spends only on riders a remedy brings back: about RM24 per extra rider kept, against RM50 for blanket discounts.乘客流失往往始于一次糟糕的计价。服务在三个时刻介入，只为补救能带回来的人花钱：每多留住一位乘客约 RM24，全员发券要 RM50。

Reframed问题重构

The problem isn't too many price shocks. It's that when one happens, the platform does nothing to recover.问题不在于价格冲击太多，而在于冲击发生之后，平台什么都没有做。

Read the notes展开文字说明
  • In Q2 2026 Grab's incentives grew 29% while GMV grew 21%; every extra US$1 of GMV now costs about 3.3¢ more in incentives than a year ago.2026 年第二季度，Grab 的补贴增长 29%，交易额只增长 21%；每多做 1 美元交易额，补贴比一年前多花约 3.3 美分。
  • Net take rate slipped from 16.0% to 15.0% and margin from 8.8% to 8.6%: efficiency is holding margins up, not pricing.净抽成率从 16.0% 降到 15.0%，利润率从 8.8% 降到 8.6%：撑住利润的是效率，不是定价。
  • 80 pieces of evidence (64% high credibility) point the same way: price shocks happen, the back end can see them, but nothing follows.80 条证据（64% 为高可信度）指向同一个结论：价格冲击确实存在，后台也能识别，但之后什么都没有发生。

02/ 07Insight洞察

Not every remedy keeps a rider不是每次补救都能留住人

No remedy: returns不补救：会回来No remedy: doesn't不补救：不回来 Remedied: returns补救后：回来
Would return anyway本来就会回来Money wasted钱白花
Returns only if remedied补救了才回来The only group worth paying for唯一值得花钱的一类
Remedied: doesn't补救后：不回来
Remedy backfires补救反而推走Money and rider both lost钱和人都丢了

How do we pay only for the riders a remedy brings back?怎样只为补救能带回来的人花钱？

Public experiments and today's policy公开实验与现行政策

  • 01Uber · 1.5M ridersUber · 150 万乘客A promo after a late trip still lifted spend +0.9% at 84 days; a third apology cut spend. At most once per 90 days.迟到后发券，84 天消费仍 +0.9%；第三次道歉反而减少消费。90 天内最多一次。
  • 02Via · New YorkVia · 纽约A $5 credit lifted spend +12% over 4 weeks and paid back in 18 days, for frequent riders only. Act fast, pick the right riders.5 美元补偿让 4 周消费 +12%，18 天回本，但只对中高频乘客有效。要快，也要挑对人。
  • 03Grab Malaysia todayGrab 马来西亚现状Cancel fee waived only when the driver is 10+ min late. Surges and driver cancellations get nothing.司机迟到 10 分钟以上才免取消费；加价和司机取消都没有任何补救。
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Riders fall into four groups: those who return anyway, those who return only if remedied, those a remedy pushes away, and those lost either way. Only the second group is worth paying for.乘客分四类：本来就会回来的、补救了才回来的、补救反而推走的、怎样都不回来的。只有第二类值得花钱。
  • Uber's 1.5M-rider experiment and Via's New York test show recovery works, but only once, fast, and for the right riders.Uber 150 万乘客实验和 Via 纽约实验都说明补救有效，但前提是只做一次、做得快、挑对人。

Three entry points, each with an owner三个介入点，各有负责团队

  • 01① Quote · pricing① 报价 · 定价团队Surge ≥ 1.8× and the rider hesitates: offer options加价 ≥ 1.8 倍且乘客犹豫：给出选择
  • 02② Pickup · dispatch② 接驾 · 调度与客服Driver 10+ min late or cancels: apologise and explain on the spot, free司机迟到 10 分钟以上或取消：当场道歉并解释，零成本
  • 03③ After the trip · CRM③ 行程后 · CRMNo ride for 1.5× the usual gap: remedy only riders who'd return超过平时间隔 1.5 倍没叫车：只补救会因此回来的人
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Across the 7 stages of a ride, emotion bottoms out at the quote, at pickup and after the trip; today the company only re-dispatches and sends blanket promo codes.一次出行有 7 个阶段，情绪在报价、接驾和行程后跌到谷底；而平台现在只会重新派单和群发优惠码。
  • Each moment has an owner and a trigger: pricing at the quote, dispatch at pickup, CRM after the trip.每个时刻都有负责团队和触发条件：报价归定价团队，接驾归调度，行程后归 CRM。

05/ 07System决策系统

Four questions before any money is spent花钱之前先回答四个问题

Price shock recovery价格冲击补救

  • Rider乘客
  • Pricing team定价团队
  • CRM teamCRM 团队
  • Ops & support运营与客服

Four conflicts, each settled by a rule, e.g. stop if driver peak earnings drop四组冲突，各由一条规则约束，如司机高峰收入下降即停止

Asked in order; any no stops it依次判断，任何一步为否即停止

  1. 01Is it a shock?算不算冲击？Surge ≥ 1.8×, 10+ min late, or cancelled加价 ≥ 1.8 倍、迟到 10 分钟以上或被取消Rule规则
  2. 02Eligible now?现在能补吗？Quiet 1.5× usual gap, none in 90 days, budget left沉默超过平时 1.5 倍、90 天内未补过、预算还有Rule规则
  3. 03Who gets it?发给谁？Rank by uplift, send to the top 10%; new riders by rule按增益排序发前 10%；新乘客按规则发AI modelAI 模型
  4. 04What do we say?怎么说？Template apology plus 'why you got this'模板道歉 + 「为什么你收到这条」LLM大模型
  5. 05Send the remedy发出补救Within 24 h: 7-day fare cap at 1.3×, about RM224 小时内：7 天车费封顶 1.3 倍，约 RM2System系统
  6. 0690-day review90 天复盘Against a random 10% held out; retune weekly对比随机留出的 10%；每周重调Results结果
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Six stakeholders, four conflicts, each settled by a rule; for example driver peak earnings per online hour is a guardrail, and sending stops if it drops.六方利益相关者、四组冲突，每组都由一条规则约束；比如司机高峰时薪是护栏，一旦下降就停止发放。
  • After a shock the system asks in order: is it a shock, is the rider eligible, who gets it, what do we say. Any 'no' stops it. Only two steps use AI.冲击发生后，系统依次判断：算不算冲击、现在能不能补、发给谁、怎么说。任何一步为否就停止，只有两步用到 AI。
