Defining Sound Boundaries,
Resonating with Light定义声音边界，
与光共振

Bone conduction and directional sound give the driver and each passenger their own "sensory bubble", while sound is turned into flowing light, so music and safety alerts share one calm language.用骨传导和定向声技术，为驾驶员和每位乘客各自营造一个「感官气泡」；同时把声音转化为流动的光，让音乐和安全提示使用同一种平静的语言。

Key insight关键洞察

Safety is not less information. It is the senses working together at the right rhythm.安全的关键不是更少的信息，而是多感官以合适的节奏协作。

Read the notes展开文字说明
  • As cars move from L2 driver assistance towards L3/L4 autonomy, the challenge shifts from controlling the car to offering richer experiences that people still trust.汽车从 L2 辅助驾驶走向 L3/L4 自动驾驶，核心挑战不再是「控制车辆」，而是在提供更丰富体验的同时让人保持对安全的信任。
  • Today's HMI is disconnected: the screen is flat while the road is three-dimensional, and the interior is physical while the interaction is digital.现有 HMI 与整体体验是割裂的：屏幕是平面的，道路是立体的；内饰是实体的，交互是数字的。
  • Key insight: driving safety is not about receiving less information, but about the senses working together at the right rhythm.关键洞察：驾驶安全的重点不在于接收的信息少，而在于多感官协作的节奏合适、分配合理。

02/ 05Framework设计框架

Three steps, from safety to courtesy三步：从安全到「数字礼仪」

  1. 01 Driving safety驾驶安全The floor every idea keeps所有设计的底线
  2. 02 Experience innovation体验创新Richer sound and light更丰富的声光体验
  3. 03 Digital gentlemanhood数字绅士Share the cabin without disturbing others共享空间，互不打扰

Grounded in理论依据

  • Neuroscience: light is noticed before sound神经科学：光比声音更快被察觉
  • Sociology社会学
  • Attention economics注意力经济
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • The framework moves from driving safety, to experience innovation, to "digital gentlemanhood": a cabin where people share space without disturbing each other.设计框架分三步：驾驶安全、体验创新，再到「数字绅士」，让同一车厢里的人互不打扰地共享空间。
  • It draws on physics and neuroscience (light is noticed faster than sound), sociology and the economics of attention.框架参考了物理与神经科学（光比声音更快被察觉）、社会学以及注意力经济的视角。

03/ 05Testing实验

Proving it inside a real car在真实车内验证

Experiments 1.1–1.5, inside a real car实验 1.1–1.5 · 在真实车内

  • 01Light & sound rig光声传导结构Built inside the vehicle在车内搭建
  • 02Direct light直射光位置Safe positions for four seats四座车的安全位置
  • 03Resonators共振器位置Felt best near the cushion靠近坐垫效果最好
  • 04Lens texture透镜纹理Soft enough not to distract the driver柔和到不干扰驾驶员
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Experiments 1.1 to 1.5 built a light-and-sound conduction rig in a vehicle, found safe positions for direct light in a four-seater, and placed resonators where each person can feel their own notes (best near the cushion).实验 1.1 至 1.5 在车内搭建光声传导结构，为四座车找到安全的直射光位置，并确定共振器的位置，让每个人都能感受到属于自己的音符（靠近坐垫效果最好）。
  • Lens textures were compared and refined until the light was soft enough not to distract the driver.对比并优化透镜纹理，直到光线柔和到不会干扰驾驶员。
