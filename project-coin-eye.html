COIN EYE:
Retinal Screening at HomeCOIN EYE：
居家视网膜筛查

A phone clip and app that reconstruct a spherical fundus image from a smartphone video, so people with diabetes can check their retinas at home without bulky equipment or pupil dilation.一个手机夹具加一款 App：用手机视频重建球面眼底图像，让糖尿病患者不用大型设备、不用散瞳，在家就能检查视网膜。

  • 1 in 101/10adults affected成年人患病比例

3 personas3 类用户画像
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • China had about 140 million people with diabetes in 2021, roughly one in ten adults. Diabetic retinopathy is an irreversible complication.2021 年中国糖尿病患者约 1.4 亿，相当于每 10 人中就有 1 人。糖尿病视网膜病变是不可逆的并发症。
  • Interviews looked at four dimensions: physical and mental health, social awareness, family care and lifestyle, and produced three personas.访谈从身心健康、社会认知、家庭照护和生活方式四个维度展开，提炼出三类用户画像。

02/ 04Pain points痛点

What people told me用户怎么说

  • “Dilated at every visit, hours to recover每次都要散瞳，恢复要很久
  • “A full check-up costs too much to repeat全面检查太贵，没法常做
  • “No daily self-check: ignore it or panic日常没法自查：要么忽视，要么焦虑

Why can't this test happen at home?为什么这项检查不能在家完成？

Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Dilation at every hospital check takes hours to recover from, and a full check-up is too expensive to repeat often.每次去医院都要散瞳，恢复要很久；全面检查费用高，难以经常做。
  • With no easy way to check day to day, patients either ignore early signs or become anxious and over-visit the hospital.日常没有方便的自查方法，患者要么忽视早期信号，要么过度焦虑、频繁跑医院。
  • So the question became: why can't this test happen at home?于是问题变成：为什么这项检查不能在家完成？

Prototype tests原型实验

  • 03Grip & eye cup握持与贴眼Comfortable size舒适尺寸

From video to diagnosis从视频到诊断

  1. 01Short video短视频
  2. 02Key frames提取关键帧
  3. 03Spherical fundus image拼接球面眼底图
  4. 04AI analysisAI 分析
Read the notes展开文字说明
  • Prototype tests covered imaging with a 20D lens, a polariser to cut glare, a comfortable grip and eye opening, a folding support and a guidance light.原型实验包括：20D 透镜成像、偏振片消除反光、握持与贴眼尺寸的舒适度、折叠支架以及引导光。
  • Key frames from a short video are stitched to widen the view towards a full spherical fundus image, then read by an AI model.从一段短视频中提取关键帧拼接，把视野扩展为完整的球面眼底图像，再交给 AI 模型分析。
